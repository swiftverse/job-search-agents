## Worth asking about
- The platform layers on top of ServiceNow and Jira Service Management. Ask how CSMs position it against, or alongside, those incumbents.
- TNW notes competitors (ServiceNow, Moveworks, Aisera) offer similar agent governance and that differentiation is unclear. Ask how customers see the difference.
- A certified AI Coworker marketplace reportedly launched around November 6, 2026 (from a search summary, not a page I opened). Ask how it changes adoption and expansion work.
- Customer counts and revenue are undisclosed. Ask about retention and expansion targets for this role.
- The posting says up to 50% travel and ANZ time zones. Ask about the customer mix in ANZ and APAC.
