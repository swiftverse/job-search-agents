"You're right, my book is 42 mid-market accounts worth $3.1M in annual recurring revenue, and I won't call them enterprise. What I can show is results. Over two years net revenue retention went from 94% to 108%, and I've run quarterly business reviews with account leaders. Do not claim enterprise or executive experience you don't have. The honest answer, plus a plan, is stronger.
