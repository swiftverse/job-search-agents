## Worth asking about
- How the AI Agent and MCP/API integrations are changing what customers need from a CSM, given the posting's AI-coaching duty.
- Customers skew toward fast-growing AI and software startups. Ask how the Mid-Market segment is defined and how churn risk is handled in that group.
- Sacra argues AI could widen the market to non-technical marketers. Ask whether the customer base is shifting that way.
- Ask how a 40-60 account portfolio is split between onboarding and expansion work, and how the APAC/EMEA team handles coverage and the language needs.
- No new funding since 2022 as far as I could find. Ask about the growth plan and the path to profitability.
