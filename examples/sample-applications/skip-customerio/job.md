# Customer Success Manager, Mid-Market (APAC or EMEA) - Customer.io

Note: posting.md is a paraphrase of the original posting, not verbatim. Wording below is approximate.

## Must-haves
- 2+ years as a CSM for technical SaaS products
- Experience communicating with software developers in a technical capacity
- Well-formed opinions on customer experience
- Located in Australia, New Zealand, the UK, or Ireland
- Fluency in French, Russian, and/or Spanish (multilingual candidates prioritized)

## Nice-to-haves
- Experience designing lifecycle messages
- Multilingual (more than one of the listed languages) is prioritized
- Comfort coaching customers on AI-powered solutions (AI-forward thinking is emphasized)

## What the job really is
You will manage a portfolio of about 40-60 Mid-Market customers. Most of your week goes to onboarding kickoffs and check-ins, building success plans, holding consultative calls, and watching health and adoption signals. You will also own escalations and work with Account Management on retention and expansion. Customer.io is hiring to keep and grow mid-market accounts in the APAC and EMEA time zones, and to get customers to measurable results on its messaging platform. The role carries commercial accountability.

## Practical details
- Location: APAC Remote or EMEA Remote, but the requirement limits applicants to Australia, New Zealand, the UK, or Ireland
- Work rights: Application asks about work authorization. No visa sponsorship or relocation support.
- Salary: Base $80,000 USD or local equivalent, depending on experience, subject to market adjustment. Stock options.
- Benefits: Full medical, dental, vision and mental health premiums for employee and family; 16 weeks paid parental leave; unlimited PTO; remote work, wellness and development stipends
- Seniority: Mid-level (2+ years as a CSM)
- Hiring process: 30-min recruiter call, 45-min hiring manager call, 45-min homework review call, then background check and employment verification for finalists
- Deadline: not stated

## Warning signs
- The language requirement is unclear. It says "French, Russian, and/or Spanish" fluency, which reads as required, yet "multilingual candidates prioritized" suggests it is flexible. Ask which it is.
- The location is listed as "APAC or EMEA" remote, but only Australia, New Zealand, the UK, and Ireland qualify. Many APAC and EMEA candidates would be screened out.
- French, Russian, and Spanish are an odd set for APAC/UK-based customers. Ask which markets you would cover.
- The hiring process includes a "homework review call", which implies a take-home assignment. The posting doesn't state its scope or whether it is paid.
- "Owning commercial outcomes" and commercial targets appear on the application form, but quotas, targets, and any variable pay are not stated. Base is "subject to market adjustment", so the local-currency figure may change.
- The posting discloses that Greenhouse AI Talent Matching assesses applications. This is a notice to applicants, not an instruction aimed at AI tools.
